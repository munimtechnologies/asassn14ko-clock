# Prior-art and scoop check, 2026-11-21

Question: has anyone published a timing analysis of ASASSN-14ko's flares after November 2021
(cycle n = 24), i.e. tested the Payne et al. constant-Pdot ephemeris on 2022-2026 flares?

Checked:
- arXiv API, all:"ASASSN-14ko", newest first (18 entries, latest 2611.21731 on 2026-11-16,
  an ASAS-SN TDE-rates paper). None refits the period.
- Payne et al. 2023 (ApJ 951, 134; arXiv:2206.11278): last timing fit, flares to Nov 2021,
  Pdot = -0.0026 +- 0.0006, forecasts for 2022-03-01 ... 2023-05-04.
- Huang et al. 2023 (arXiv:2310.03236): Swift UV/optical of epochs 10-11; quotes the
  Payne Pdot, no refit. Its text gives UV peaks near MJD 59962 and 60070, i.e. the n = 28 and
  n = 29 forecasts (59962.1, 60069.0) were met in the UV (not presented as a test).
- Huang et al. 2025 (ApJ; arXiv:2506.15039): data to MJD 60540 (Aug 2024), 15 epochs; adopts
  P = 115.2 d and Pdot = -0.0026; no timing update, no O-C.
- Theory citing the Pdot as open: Linial & Quataert 2024 (arXiv:2309.15849), Bandopadhyay et
  al. 2024 (arXiv:2406.03675), Wu et al. 2024 (arXiv:2402.18812), Yao et al. 2025
  (arXiv:2505.10611), Cufari et al. 2022 (arXiv:2203.08162).
- Web search for 2025-2026 ATels or notes on ASASSN-14ko timing: none found.

Conclusion: the 2022-2026 period evolution (about 15 flares) is unpublished. The ASAS-SN
team (Payne, Shappee, Hinkle, Kochanek, Stanek) and the USTC group (Huang, Wang, Jiang) hold
the deepest data and could publish at any time; re-check right before publishing results.
